Bridging Brazilian Alchemy with Philippine Artistry

Glossy Strand Hair Care Products Trading is the sole and exclusive country distributor of Sarah K Professional Brazil in the Philippines. Originally manufactured in the cosmetic heartland of Brazil, Sarah K is globally celebrated across more than 50 nations for its groundbreaking nanoplastia, taninoplasty blonde smoothing, and molecular capillary reconstruction technologies.

Established in year 2025 and headquartered at Red Corner Building, Corner Mabini, Delgado St. Iloilo City, Philippines 5000, Glossy Strand was founded to solve a critical industry challenge: providing Filipino hairstylists, salon owners, and beauty clinics with 100% legitimate, verifiable, formaldehyde-free Brazilian formulations that protect both client wellness and stylist longevity.

Led by Catalina Posada, the owner and CEO, our organization ensures that every bottle imported into the Philippines carries authentic batch traceability, genuine laboratory seals, and full Philippine FDA cosmetic notification compliance.
